## 2. Logo

### Primary Logo

The 308 Digital wordmark combines a bold, geometric numeral lockup ("308") with a dispersing pixel motif that fades from solid orange to charcoal — symbolizing data transforming into intelligence. The word "DIGITAL" sits below in a clean, spaced geometric sans-serif, anchored by a small orange triangular accent.

### Clear Space & Minimum Size

- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the pixel motif on all sides — no text, imagery, or edges should intrude.
- Minimum digital size: 32px height. Minimum print size: 20mm height.
- Do not place the logo smaller than the point where the pixel motif becomes illegible.

### Logo Usage — Do

- Use the full-color version on white or light (Cloud White / Fog Grey) backgrounds.
- Use a single-color (white or charcoal) reversed version on dark or busy backgrounds.
- Scale the logo proportionally at all times.

### Logo Usage — Don't

- Don't stretch, skew, or rotate the logo.
- Don't recolor the logo outside the approved palette.
- Don't place the full-color logo on backgrounds with insufficient contrast.
- Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or effects beyond the original artwork.
- Don't separate the pixel motif, the "308" mark, and the "DIGITAL" wordmark from one another.

## 3. Color Palette

Our palette pairs energetic orange — signaling momentum, innovation, and intelligence in motion — with a grounded slate charcoal that conveys trust, precision, and technical credibility.

| Name | Hex | RGB | Primary Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ignition Orange | `#FE6A01` | 254, 106, 1 | Logo, CTAs, links, accents, highlights |
| Deep Orange | `#FE4C01` | 254, 76, 1 | Gradient depth, hover states |
| Slate Charcoal | `#454E57` | 69, 78, 87 | Logo, body text, headers |
| Deep Charcoal | `#3F4851` | 63, 72, 81 | Dark backgrounds, footers, primary text |
| Cloud White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Backgrounds, negative space, reversed text |
| Fog Grey | `#F2F3F4` | 242, 243, 244 | Section backgrounds, cards, dividers |

### Usage Ratio

- Approx. 60% white / light grey (space, clarity), 30% charcoal (text, structure), 10% orange (accents, CTAs, emphasis).
- Orange should always feel like an accent or highlight — never the dominant field color across large areas.

## 4. Typography

### Typeface Style

The logo's "DIGITAL" wordmark uses a bold, geometric, evenly-spaced sans-serif. Website and collateral typography should follow the same geometric-sans character.

**Recommended headings:** Poppins, Montserrat, or Sora — Bold / SemiBold, generous letter-spacing for uppercase labels.

**Recommended body copy:** Inter, Source Sans Pro, or system UI sans — Regular / Medium, for readability at small sizes.

### Hierarchy Guidance

- H1 / Hero: Bold, 40–56px, Charcoal or Orange accent word
- H2 / Section: SemiBold, 28–32px, Deep Charcoal
- H3 / Subhead: SemiBold, 20–22px, Deep Orange
- Body: Regular, 16–18px, Slate Charcoal, 1.5–1.6 line height
- Labels / Eyebrow text: Medium, 12–13px, uppercase, letter-spaced, Orange or Mid Grey
